# Assemble the platform experiences

*Adults in an acute moment and creators each have their journey: teachers who have to take over a class at short notice, parents who notice that their child struggles with something they cannot help with, and creators, because supply does not come by itself (D4). Each experience takes one role's point of view in one core relationship (D4) and orders transactions (D5) and services (D6) into a journey.*

Adults rarely plan ahead: they look for an app when they need it now. What they feel is relief, not excitement: "it fits and it runs right away". This shapes every step: trust has to be built in rather than checked, one app counts more than a collection, and anything asked afterwards must take a single click.

In every journey, the thank-you after use is a step of its own. The platform is free, and thanks is what keeps it alive: every user should understand this, without the platform being pushy about it.

## An app in class within minutes

The journey of a teacher who has to take over a class at short notice, for a colleague and perhaps outside their own subject: find an app for this topic and grade, see at a glance that it may be used, put it on the projector or the class's devices, and say thanks afterwards. It may be used because the platform lists only apps that pass its checks of fitness for use and serves each one itself, under lernapps.net and its privacy notice (KD-21).

## Understand a topic at one's own pace

The journey of a parent who notices that their child struggles with a topic and cannot help, for lack of time or because they do not know the subject well enough: find an app that helps the child understand that topic, with no scope set from outside, let the child start right away and practise, and say thanks afterwards. The child keeps coming back to the app, or finds the next one alone, for as long as it needs; the child may also be the one who notices the gap. The app starts from lernapps.net like every listed app (KD-21), so parents do not have to judge a stranger's address.

## List an app and hear it is used

The journey of the supply side: a creator who built an app for their own class with their AI assistant lists it with almost no effort and then hears whether and how it helped elsewhere. Creators do not start for the reach, but use and praise keep them going (D4). The thanks they receive come from the one click every user is asked for. The platform serves a copy of the app under lernapps.net (KD-21), so the creator stays the author but no longer answers to schools for data protection; in return the app needs a licence that allows the copy.

## Practise a given scope, made to measure

The journey of a creator for whom an occasion sets exactly what to practise and no app covers it, such as a parent before their child's test or a teacher who needs exercises for a substitute lesson. It often follows “Understand a topic at one's own pace”, when a test comes up and the general app does not cover its material. The photos of school material go to the assistant only. What proved itself continues as in “List an app and hear it is used”.
