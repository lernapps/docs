The concepts behind lernapps.net

Why lernapps.net exists, how the platform is meant to work and who carries it. All open, all as models that people read and agents query.

The vision

Many small solutions. One real change.

AI makes it easy to build exactly the learning app that is needed right now: personal software. Mostly it stays where it was made. lernapps.net turns these one-offs into a change for many: easy to find, free to use and built so that no one has to worry.

  • Made where it is neededBuilt with AI by people who know the subject and the learners.
  • Reaches far beyondWhat helped once finds its way to everyone facing the same task.
  • Used without worryNo account, no ads, nothing collected in secret. Checked, not just promised.
