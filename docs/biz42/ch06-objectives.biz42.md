# Chapter 6: Objectives

The objectives follow the two stages of the platform design's MVP (D8): a first draft with the creators the owner knows, then the real MVP with teachers who still have to be won. Dates are relative to the start of the first draft (October 2026).

## First apps listed

Creators list an app when it costs minutes. Without apps there is nothing to find and nothing to thank for. Target: within two weeks of inviting them.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-first-apps
title: First apps listed by the owner's creators, within two weeks of the invitation
owner: owner-platform
addresses: risk-weak-supply, risk-invisibility
measured-by: measure-apps-listed
requires: cap-listing, cap-communication
:::
```

## Thanks arrive

Already in the first draft, thanks should arrive without anyone explaining the thank-you: the platform has to make it self-evident. Target: within four weeks of the first listings.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-thanks
title: Thanks arrive for listed apps without being explained, within four weeks
owner: owner-platform
addresses: risk-no-thanks, risk-weak-supply
measured-by: measure-thanks
requires: cap-thanks
:::
```

## Apps used in class through the platform alone

The platform has to convince teachers on its own: a referral link, passed on by the owner's contacts to colleagues the owner does not know, must lead to use in class. Target: in the first draft's referral wave, then about 10 teachers in the real MVP.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-teacher-use
title: Teachers reached by referral use apps in class, first wave in the first draft
owner: owner-platform
addresses: risk-reach, risk-unsafe-adoption, opp-pisa-window
measured-by: measure-teacher-use
requires: cap-communication, cap-listing
:::
```

## Trust built in

Every listed app meets the conditions for listing, and what an app loads is checked, not taken on trust. Target: the check runs for every listing before the real MVP.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-trust
title: Every listed app checked against the conditions for listing, before the real MVP
owner: owner-platform
addresses: risk-dsgvo, risk-endorsement, opp-fitness-signal
measured-by: measure-fitness-checked
requires: cap-fitness-check
:::
```

## Guidance for building

Creators get guidance their AI assistant can follow, so that apps are built faster and work for others too: a benefit independent of reach. Target: before the real MVP.

```biz42
:::objective
id: obj-agent-guidance
title: Guidance for creators' AI assistants published, before the real MVP
owner: owner-platform
addresses: risk-weak-supply, opp-ai-creation
measured-by: measure-guidance-use
requires: cap-guidance
:::
```
