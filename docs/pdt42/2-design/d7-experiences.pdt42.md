# Assemble the platform experiences

*Draft for discussion. Each experience takes one role's point of view in one core relationship (D4) and orders transactions (D5) and services (D6) into a journey. The first is the core entity's; the second is the creators', because supply does not come by itself (D4).*

## An app for next week

The journey of the core entity: an adult looking for an app for one topic and grade, who brings it to class and gives something back. It covers onboarding and getting better; adapting apps is a later, transformative experience.

```pdt42
:::canvas
id: cv-x-app-for-next-week
canvas: platform-experience
of: x-app-for-next-week
:::
```

```pdt42
:::experience
id: x-app-for-next-week
title: An app for next week
core-entity: e-adopters
roles: e-creators, e-students
relationship: r-creators-adults
value-proposition: An active-learning app for your topic and grade that you can use next week: no doubt about whether it may be used, no installation, no sign-up
steps: s-finding, t-find-app, s-fitness-clarity, t-confirm-fitness, s-class-sets, t-bring-app, t-use-in-class, s-giving-back, t-give-feedback, t-thank-creator-adult
activities:
  - Keeping listings comparable and current
  - Keeping the signal of fitness for use reliable
resources:
  - The listings
  - The signal of fitness for use
costs:
  - The platform owner's time
  - Near-zero operating costs, as everything runs in the browser
revenues:
  - No revenue: the platform is non-profit
  - Value captured as use, feedback and thanks that keep creators contributing
:::
```

## List an app and hear it is used

The journey of the supply side: a creator who built an app for their own class lists it with almost no effort and then learns that and how it is used elsewhere. Reach is no reason to start, but use and praise keep creators (D4).

```pdt42
:::canvas
id: cv-x-list-and-hear-back
canvas: platform-experience
of: x-list-and-hear-back
:::
```

```pdt42
:::experience
id: x-list-and-hear-back
title: List an app and hear it is used
core-entity: e-creators
roles: e-adopters
relationship: r-creators-adults
value-proposition: Build your app faster with good guidance, list it in minutes, and hear when it helps a class beyond your own
steps: s-building-guidance, s-listing-help, t-list-app, t-use-in-class, s-use-insight, t-give-feedback, t-recommend-app, t-thank-creator-adult
activities:
  - Maintaining the guidance for building apps
  - Checking and publishing listings
resources:
  - The guidance for building apps
  - The listings
costs:
  - The platform owner's time, later shared with co-owners (D6)
  - Near-zero operating costs
revenues:
  - No revenue: the platform is non-profit
  - Value captured as more and better apps for adults and students
:::
```
