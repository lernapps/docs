# Chapter 8: Owners

## Platform owner

Oliver Jägle is accountable for the platform: all strategic objectives and products. Ralf D. Müller, the second owner of the GitHub organisation lernapps, focuses on the apps themselves, such as the Mathe-Karte, and does not take part in the platform. App repositories belong to their authors. There is no registered legal entity yet; if the platform takes off, it could become a registered association (Verein), so that it belongs to those who fill it with life. How decisions are made is described in [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/lernapps/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md); it still describes the two owners with equal rights on all common repositories.
