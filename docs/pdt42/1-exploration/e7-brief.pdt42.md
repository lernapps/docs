## WHAT-IF scenarios

### The one app for the one gap is found

Every app teaches one aspect, and demand is just as fine-grained: a teacher looks for something on exactly one topic for exactly one grade. Today these niches never meet.

```pdt42
:::scenario
id: sc-niches-meet
title: The one app for the one gap is found
pattern: e3
arena: ar-into-use
impact: A teacher looking for one topic in one grade finds the app someone built for exactly that gap
:::
```

### Apps that work in class rise

Many apps are built and used once. As attention for contributed apps is the scarce resource, it has to go to the apps that work: feedback from classroom use and forks show which ones do.

```pdt42
:::scenario
id: sc-best-emerge
title: Apps that work in class rise
pattern: e8
arena: ar-into-use
impact: Feedback from classroom use and forks direct the scarce attention to the apps that work
:::
```

### Teacher platforms show apps from lernapps.net

Adults already find apps on teacher platforms such as fobizz or Serlo. Instead of competing with them, lernapps.net lets them show its apps, so the platforms become a channel for contributed apps. This is likely limited to non-profit platforms such as Serlo: for commercial ones, free apps are rather unwelcome competition (D3).

```pdt42
:::scenario
id: sc-platforms-as-channels
title: Teacher platforms show apps from lernapps.net
pattern: e12
arena: ar-into-use
impact: Non-profit teacher platforms become a channel for contributed apps instead of a competing offering
:::
```

## Attention for small learning apps

The platformization space is the focus arena "Getting apps into use". The core relationship is between app creators and the adults who bring apps into use; students play an ancillary role, as they usually get to know an app through an adult. The brief collects what E3 to E6 found: the transactions to standardise, the traits of the product side, and the moat to reckon with.

```pdt42
:::brief
id: br-attention
title: Attention for small learning apps
arena: ar-into-use
entities: e-creators, e-adopters, e-students
standardize:
  - Listing an app (what it does, for whom)
  - Passing an app on by link or QR code
  - Signalling fitness for use
product-side:
  - Guidance for AI assistants
  - Starter templates
  - Frontend-only hosting
  - Personalisation without accounts
moats: mo-data-protection
:::
```
